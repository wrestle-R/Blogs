# Guitar reductions used by the prototype

These are synthesized guitar reductions, not studio recordings or claims of note-for-note production reproduction. 43 songs are eligible as quiz questions: the five original reductions, Game of Thrones and 37 additional source-referenced excerpts. The catalogue contains 83 titles from the owner’s requests and the existing Spotify endpoint’s 50 short-term top tracks (retrieved 2026-10-23); remaining titles are distractors, pending usable timed notation.

This is a guitar reduction, not the orchestral recording; no claim of human listening verification.


Do not label this bank “ekdum accurate” or fully audio-verified. Browser and waveform tests verify scheduling, pitch generation and interaction, not perceptual identity with a recording. Songs without a reviewed score must not be added as playable placeholders. The remaining catalogue requires transcription and timing checks.

## Expanded bank (2026-10-23)

`guitar-arrangements.json` stores short excerpts, not complete transcriptions. Each entry identifies the contributor’s Songsterr song ID, exact revision, instrument part and measures. These public, unrestricted contributor tabs are source references, not official artist scores. Entries labelled AI-generated by the source were excluded; this does not independently certify every contributor note. No audio-by-ear comparison with the original recordings is claimed.

Rational source durations include dots and tuplets; ties extend a note rather than retrigger it. Leading empty bars are skipped. Each excerpt is bounded to 16 quarter beats or 12 seconds, whichever is shorter. Pitch classes and intervals remain intact: one whole-octave shift is used across each phrase to fit the guitar. Notes sharing an onset retain the chord; duplicated unisons are merged, and each chord uses distinct strings. Homecoming omits the piano’s bass below MIDI 48. Guitar sound replaces the source instrument; bends, slides, vibrato, lyrics and production effects are omitted. Live Mind Over Matter follows the cited live guitar arrangement. These are short guitar reductions, not promises of exact studio timbre or articulation.

The first five arrangements and GoT retain their earlier references above. Expanded source revision IDs are deliberately pinned so upstream edits cannot silently alter the quiz. Runtime playback uses local note data and does not call Songsterr or Spotify.
