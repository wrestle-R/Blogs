# Guitar reductions used by the prototype

These are synthesized guitar reductions, not studio recordings or claims of note-for-note production reproduction. Only five owner-selected songs are currently eligible as quiz questions. Other catalogue entries are distractors, pending source and timing review.

- **Shape of You**: short C-sharp minor ostinato. Pitch reference: CCYD Music Year 8 Ostinato booklet, https://www.ccyd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/YEAR-8-OSTINATO-BOOKLET.pdf (search-index reference; source URL currently unavailable). This legacy arrangement needs independent rhythm comparison before it can be called recording-accurate.
- **blue**: Patricia Reichel's [score](https://www.kalimbaclass.com/tutoriales-para-kalimba/blue-yung-kai-tabs-tutorial-sheet-music-partitura), page 2 bars 1–4. C-major score moved to E major and down an octave for guitar. 12/8, dotted-quarter 70 equals quarter-note 105; eighth notes occupy half a scheduler beat. Melodic line only, backing harmony omitted.
- **Dandelions**: Patricia Reichel's [score](https://www.kalimbaclass.com/tutoriales-para-kalimba/dandelions-ruth-berhe-tutorials), page 2 bars 1–4. C-major reduction moved to D-flat and down an octave. Quarter 80; triplets use exact thirds, not rounded eighth notes. Melody only.
- **Snowman**: Patricia Reichel's [score](https://www.kalimbaclass.com/tutoriales-para-kalimba/showman-sia), page 2 bars 1–6. C-major reduction moved to D-flat and down an octave. Dotted-quarter 69 equals quarter 103.5. Intro arpeggios, rest, vocal pickup and opening phrase retained. This arrangement's tempo differs slightly from the original's 105 quarter-note marking; not presented as exact original timing.
- **505**: Music Will's [guitar chart](https://jamzone.musicwill.org/Songchart/Guitar-505-ArcticMonkeys.pdf) and [Andy Guitar](https://www.andyguitar.co.uk/videos/aaa-live-fraser-arctic-monkeys-251124?context=home-live-replays). Four-bar Dm–Dm–Em–Em opening at quarter 140; reduced sustained chord voicings, without the original organ's timbre. Chord identity is verified; exact organ articulation is not transcribed.
- **Game of Thrones**: [Un Pianiste arranger score](https://unpianiste.com/wp-content/uploads/partitions/Game_of_Thrones.pdf). Main-title melody lowered one octave, long tied notes gently replucked for guitar sustain. A quiet independently arranged arpeggio accompanies it. Used for the homepage endurance Easter egg, not the owner's new pop-song quiz pool. Full guitar reduction, not the orchestral recording.

Do not label this bank “ekdum accurate” or fully audio-verified. Browser and waveform tests verify scheduling, pitch generation and interaction, not perceptual identity with a recording. Songs without a reviewed score must not be added as playable placeholders. The remaining owner-selected catalogue requires transcription and timing checks.
